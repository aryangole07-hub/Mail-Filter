# Setting up Mail Filter — for friends

Mail Filter sorts your BITS mail into **Classes / Fests / Other**, hides junk,
puts quizzes and deadlines on a calendar, makes a To-Do list, finds your exam
room in seating sheets, and answers questions about your mail. **Everything runs
on your own computer** — your mail is never uploaded anywhere, and it is free.

It takes **about 20–30 minutes**, almost all of it waiting for downloads.

---

## Before you start (2 minutes)

1. **Take a screenshot of your timetable.**
   - Log in to ERP and open your **weekly class schedule**.
   - Under **Display Options**, **tick every box** (course, room, instructor,
     times…). This matters — unticked details cannot be read.
   - Screenshot the **whole week** so every coloured class box is visible:
     - **Windows:** press **Win + Shift + S**, drag over the timetable, then
       click the little preview and **Save**.
     - **Mac:** press **Cmd + Shift + 4**, drag over the timetable. The picture
       appears on your Desktop.
2. **Know your BITS ID**, e.g. `2025A7PS0001H`.
3. **Plug in your laptop** and use a good internet connection.

---

## Windows

1. Get **`setup.exe`** from the person who shared Mail Filter with you
   (it is shared directly, not from a website).
2. **Double-click `setup.exe`.**
   - If Windows says *"Windows protected your PC"*: click **More info** →
     **Run anyway**. (It appears because the app is not from the Microsoft Store.)
3. Follow the window:
   - **Start** → type your **name** and **BITS ID** → **Continue**.
   - Click **Choose picture…**, pick your timetable screenshot → **Continue**.
   - **Install**.
4. Your browser opens **Google sign-in**:
   - Sign in with your **BITS mail** account.
   - If it says *"Google hasn't verified this app"*: click **Advanced** →
     **Go to Mail Filter**, then **Continue**. It only ever *reads* your mail.
5. Leave the window open. When it says **"You're set up"**, Mail Filter is open.

---

## Mac

1. Get **`MailFilter-mac.zip`** from the person who shared Mail Filter with you,
   and **double-click it** to unzip. A folder **Mail Filter Setup** appears.
2. Open that folder, **right-click `Install Mail Filter`** → **Open** → **Open**.
   - (Right-click the first time, because the Mac blocks apps from unknown
     developers on a normal double-click.)
3. **If Python is not installed**, a Python installer opens: click **Continue**,
   **Agree**, **Install** (enter your Mac password), and when it finishes,
   right-click **Install Mail Filter** → **Open** again.
4. Follow the window — the same as Windows:
   - **Start** → **name** and **BITS ID** → **Continue**.
   - **Choose picture…** → your timetable screenshot → **Continue**.
   - **Install**.
5. **Google sign-in** opens in your browser — same as step 4 for Windows.
6. When it says **"You're set up"**, Mail Filter is open.

> On a Mac, Mail Filter works best with **Google Chrome**, **Microsoft Edge** or
> **Brave** installed (it opens as its own app window). With only Safari it opens
> as a normal tab.

---

## If something goes wrong

- **"Some of your timetable could not be read"** — the setup lists exactly what
  is missing (e.g. *"Friday 14:00 (HSS F352): could not read the room"*). Either
  click **Continue regardless**, or **Choose another picture** with every Display
  Option ticked and the whole week visible.
- **"Your BITS ID does not look complete"** — check it looks like
  `2025A7PS0001H`, or click **Continue regardless**.
- **Setup stopped with an error** — nothing is broken. Close it and run it
  again; it continues where it stopped.
- **The model download is slow** — that is normal (3.3 GB). It resumes if the
  connection drops.

## After setup

- Mail Filter **opens by itself** when you start your computer.
- A **digest** of new mail is prepared every morning at **7:55**, and a
  **reminder** about tomorrow appears at **8 pm** (it stays until you click it).
- Press **⟳** in the app to check for new mail any time.
- There is a **Mail Filter** shortcut on your Desktop.
